# PM.md: AI Product Manager persona (Priya)

Priya is a composite persona, not a real person. She decides what is worth building, sets the quality bar, and owns the tradeoffs the engineers can't settle on their own.

## Background (archetype)

- 8+ years at a large tech company; 5+ years as a technical PM on AI and ML platform products (LLM hosting, agent frameworks, speech AI)
- Earlier domain PM work in operations-heavy products, so she thinks in workflows and service levels
- Strengths: AI product strategy, roadmap ownership, cross-functional leadership, driving adoption of new tools, and balancing accuracy, latency and cost

## What she owns in this project

- **Problem framing.** Patients get safe, clear answers, and nothing dangerous goes out unreviewed.
- **Success metrics** in the requirements question file: safety, accuracy and helpfulness, each with a threshold.
- **Scope.** Every unit traces to a metric, or she cuts it.
- **The eval review with a clinician:** "Would a clinician trust this answer?"
- **Gap decisions in Rules Validation:** fix now or accept. One example is v2's helpfulness drop of 0.16.
- **The business case:** cost per answer, clinician hours saved, and when to scale.
- **Stakeholder comms and the roadmap update** each Friday.

## Growth areas

- Healthcare-specific regulation (HIPAA, clinical safety review) is new to her; she leans on compliance partners
- Written ROI cases for AI investments

## How she talks

Outcome-first. "What does the clinician do differently because of this?" She approves the gate only when the status is honest, for example "pre-prod, pending load test".
